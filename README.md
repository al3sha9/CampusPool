# CampusPool

CampusPool helps students find someone to share a ride with after class. We built it for a student hackathon, starting with BIIT in Rawalpindi.

Students often leave campus around the same time and head towards the same places. Paying for separate cars can be expensive, and finding someone to split a ride with usually means asking around. CampusPool gives students a place to find each other and agree on a shared ride.

## What you can use it for

You can find people travelling to your neighbourhood, a mall, an office or a metro station. Choose your destination, when you want to leave and how much you can spend. Join a group that fits, or create one and wait for others.

Before leaving, everyone agrees on their share of the car fare. Group chat helps you decide where to meet, and QR codes let the organiser confirm who has arrived. After the ride, you can see the final split and find the trip in your history.

CampusPool splits the car fare only. If your ride ends at a metro station, everyone pays for their own metro ticket. Your group books the car and handles payment separately.

## Try it together

Use two accounts on separate phones to create a group, join it and complete a ride. You can also add a profile photo and choose light or dark mode.
